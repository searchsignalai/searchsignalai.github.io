What does AEO involve?

Answer Engine Optimization makes useful information easier for answer-focused systems to identify and present. It relies on clear questions, direct answers and well-organised supporting detail.

Answer the question early

Put a concise, complete answer near the start of the relevant section. Follow it with context, evidence and practical detail.

Use question-led headings

Write headings that reflect real user questions. Keep each section focused on one clear intent.

Choose readable formats

Use short paragraphs, ordered steps, comparison tables and definitions when they make the answer easier to understand.

Add supporting context

Explain terms, identify entities and keep important facts consistent. Structured data can help describe the page, but must match visible content.

Earn trust

Show who created the content, cite reliable sources where appropriate and keep information accurate and current.

How SEO, AEO and GEO work together

SEO provides the discovery foundation. AEO makes answers easier to identify and use. GEO focuses on the clarity, reliability and context that can help generative systems reference information. These practices overlap, and the right mix depends on your audience and website.
