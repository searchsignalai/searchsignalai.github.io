ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION

Make useful information easy to answer.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on structuring information so people and answer-focused systems can identify clear, relevant responses to specific questions. It builds on useful content, clear language and sound SEO foundations.

AEO BUILDING BLOCKS

Structure content around real questions.

AEO is not a special markup trick. It is a content and information-design approach that makes answers easier to find and understand.

ANSWER DESIGN

Give readers the answer, then the detail.

A useful answer section should be direct without being shallow. Start with the core response, explain why it is true, and add steps or examples that help the reader act.

EXAMPLE CONTENT STRUCTURE

What is AEO?

Short answer: AEO is the practice of organising useful content so direct answers to user questions are clear and easy to identify.

Then explain: Include context, examples, important limitations and references that support the answer.

Does schema markup guarantee answer visibility?

No. Structured data can help describe entities and page content, but it does not guarantee that a search engine or answer platform will select or display a response. Markup must be accurate and reflect content that visitors can see.

How to improve a page for answer engines

  1. Identify the main question and related questions behind the search intent.
  2. Answer the main question clearly near the top of the relevant section.
  3. Use descriptive H2 and H3 headings to organise related points.
  4. Add examples, definitions, steps or tables when they improve understanding.
  5. Check facts, cite suitable sources and keep the page updated.
  6. Link to related pages that provide useful additional context.
