QUICK ANSWERS

Questions about the new search era.

Start with the basics, then explore each topic in more detail.

Are SEO, AEO and GEO the same thing?

No. SEO focuses on search discovery, AEO focuses on direct answers, and GEO focuses on visibility in generative search experiences. They overlap and can support one another.

Does SEO still matter for AI search?

Yes. Accessible pages, useful content and sound technical foundations remain important. AI search does not make good SEO irrelevant.

Does structured data guarantee AI citations?

No. Structured data can help clarify page information, but it does not guarantee rankings, rich results or citations in AI-generated answers.

Where should I start with SEO, AEO and GEO?

Start with a crawlable website and genuinely useful content. Then improve question-focused answers, source quality, authorship and topic clarity.
