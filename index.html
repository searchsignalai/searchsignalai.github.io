Are SEO, AEO and GEO the same thing?+

No. SEO supports discovery in search engines. AEO focuses on clear responses to questions. GEO focuses on making useful, well-supported information easier to understand and reference in generative search experiences.

Does SEO still matter for AI search?+

Yes. Search accessibility, crawlable pages, sound technical foundations and helpful content remain important. Good SEO does not guarantee an AI citation, but it supports the discoverability of your information.

How can content become more useful for answer engines?+

Answer real questions directly, use descriptive headings, explain specialist terms, support claims with reliable sources and keep information accurate and easy to navigate.

Does schema markup guarantee AI recommendations?+

No. Structured data helps describe the content and entities on a page. It does not guarantee rankings, rich results, AI citations or recommendations.

Where should a beginner start with AI search optimization?+

Start with technical SEO and useful content. Then improve question coverage, page structure, source quality, author information and consistency across your website.
