Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are SEO, AEO and GEO the same thing?

No. SEO supports visibility in search engines, AEO focuses on direct answers, and GEO focuses on making content understandable and referenceable in generative search experiences.

Does SEO still matter for AI search?

Yes. Accessible pages, sound technical foundations and useful content help systems discover and assess information. AI visibility does not replace the need for good SEO.

Does schema guarantee AI citations?

No. Structured data can clarify what a page or entity represents, but it does not guarantee rankings, rich results or citations.

Where should I start?

Start by making your site crawlable, publishing genuinely useful content and answering your audience's real questions. Then improve sourcing, authorship and entity consistency.
