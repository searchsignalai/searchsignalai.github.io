THE CONTEXT LAYER

Make every claim understandable and checkable.

Useful generative-search content gives a reader enough context to understand what is being claimed, who or what the claim refers to, and where supporting evidence can be found.

Is GEO different from SEO?

GEO and SEO overlap, but they describe different areas of focus. SEO covers the wider work of helping search engines discover, crawl, index and rank useful pages. GEO focuses on how content is presented with enough clarity, evidence and context to be useful in generative search experiences. Strong SEO foundations remain important, but they do not guarantee AI citations.

Practical GEO steps for a website

  1. Publish original information that directly addresses a clear audience need.
  2. Use consistent entity names and explain important relationships.
  3. Support factual claims with suitable sources and dates.
  4. Make authorship and editorial responsibility clear where appropriate.
  5. Use structured data only when it accurately describes visible page content.
  6. Keep key pages current and connect them with relevant internal links.
