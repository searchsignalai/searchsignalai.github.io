What does GEO involve?

Generative Engine Optimization focuses on making content easier for generative search systems to retrieve, interpret and potentially reference. No technique guarantees AI citations.

Publish verifiable facts

Use precise statements, current figures and named sources. Make it possible for readers to check important claims.

Make entities clear

Use consistent names for your organisation, products, authors and services. Explain relationships instead of relying on vague references.

Show expertise and authorship

Identify the author, explain relevant experience and provide editorial or review details when they add real value.

Structure information clearly

Use descriptive headings, concise definitions, lists and tables where they help people understand the topic.

Build a trusted web presence

Keep your site and important profiles consistent. Earn relevant mentions through useful work, not artificial citation schemes.

How SEO, AEO and GEO work together

SEO provides the discovery foundation. AEO makes answers easier to identify and use. GEO focuses on the clarity, reliability and context that can help generative systems reference information. These practices overlap, and the right mix depends on your audience and website.
