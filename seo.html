What does SEO involve?

Search Engine Optimization helps search engines discover, crawl, understand and show useful pages for relevant searches.

Make pages accessible

Check that important pages can be crawled, return the right status codes, load reliably and are not blocked by accidental noindex directives.

Match search intent

Research the language people use, then create pages that satisfy the reason behind the query. Avoid forcing keywords where they do not belong.

Build helpful content

Cover the topic accurately and clearly. Use descriptive headings, original insight, examples and updated information.

Strengthen site structure

Use logical navigation, descriptive URLs and relevant internal links so people and crawlers can discover related pages.

Measure and improve

Use Search Console and analytics to review indexing, impressions, clicks and conversions. Improve pages based on evidence.

How SEO, AEO and GEO work together

SEO provides the discovery foundation. AEO makes answers easier to identify and use. GEO focuses on the clarity, reliability and context that can help generative systems reference information. These practices overlap, and the right mix depends on your audience and website.
