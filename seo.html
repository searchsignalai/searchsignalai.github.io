What does effective SEO include?

SEO combines technical accessibility, content relevance, site structure and ongoing measurement. No single tactic can guarantee a ranking.

⌕

Keyword research

Understand the language people use, the intent behind searches and the topics your pages need to cover.

⌘

Technical SEO

Help crawlers access important pages with sound status codes, internal links, mobile usability and reliable performance.

▤

Helpful content

Create original, accurate pages that answer the searcher’s need with clear structure and meaningful detail.

◇

On-page SEO

Use descriptive titles, headings, URLs, image alt text and relevant internal links to explain page topics.

⇄

Site architecture

Organise related pages into a logical structure so visitors and search engines can find important information.

◷

Measurement

Review indexing, impressions, clicks and conversions in tools such as Google Search Console and analytics platforms.

How SEO supports AEO and GEO

SEO supports the discovery layer of a website. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) adds a focus on clear, direct answers to questions. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on presenting accurate facts, clear entities and useful context that generative systems may retrieve or reference. These practices overlap, but none guarantees a position, answer placement or AI citation.

Common SEO mistakes to avoid

  • Creating pages that target keywords but do not satisfy search intent.
  • Publishing near-duplicate pages with little unique value.
  • Blocking important content from crawling or indexing by mistake.
  • Ignoring internal links, mobile usability or page experience.
  • Using structured data that does not match visible page content.
