Important: This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Review and adjust it if your website tools, forms, analytics or data practices change.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you contact SearchSignal AI through the Google Form linked on our contact page, you may be asked to provide information such as your name, email address, subject or message. Only submit information relevant to your enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

2. How contact information may be used

Information you submit may be used to review and respond to your enquiry, handle feedback or corrections, and communicate with you about the request you made. We do not claim to sell personal information. We do not use contact details for unrelated purposes unless you have agreed or the law permits it.

3. Google Forms and third-party services

Our contact page links to a Google Form. When you open or submit that form, Google may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. Responses are stored in the form owner’s Google account and may also be available in a linked Google Sheet if one is configured. Review Google’s privacy information before submitting personal information.

4. Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The platform may process technical request information to deliver, maintain and protect the website. For details about platform-level processing, consult GitHub’s current privacy statement.

5. Cookies, analytics and embedded content

If analytics, advertising, embedded videos or other third-party tools are added, this policy should be updated to explain the information those tools collect and how visitors can manage their choices. Third-party services may set their own cookies or similar technologies.

6. External links

Our pages may link to external websites, articles and services. We do not control their content, security or privacy practices. Check the relevant provider’s policies before sharing information with them.

7. Data security and retention

We take a reasonable approach to handling enquiries, but no website or online service can promise absolute security. Information submitted through third-party forms may remain in the form owner’s account until it is deleted or managed by that account owner, subject to the provider’s systems and policies.

8. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit information through the contact form. If you have a question about an enquiry you submitted, use our contact page. Requests involving data held by Google may need to be made through the relevant Google account or service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website or its data practices change. The updated version will be published here. Please review this page from time to time.
