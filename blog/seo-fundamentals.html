SEO Fundamentals: A Practical Starting Guide

A practical introduction to SEO, from crawlability and keyword research to useful content, internal links and measurement.

Practical guideSearch visibilityUpdated 2026

What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization is the process of improving a website so search engines can discover, understand and show its pages for relevant searches. It combines technical accessibility, useful content and signals that help establish relevance and trust.

Start with crawlability and indexing

Make sure important pages can be reached through links, return appropriate HTTP status codes and are not blocked by robots directives. Submit a sitemap and use Search Console to monitor indexing. Submission does not guarantee indexing.

Research keywords and intent

Look beyond search volume. Identify what the searcher wants to learn, compare or do. Group related queries by intent and create a page that satisfies the need without repeating keywords unnaturally.

Create genuinely useful content

Use clear headings, answer the main question early and include examples or original information. Keep claims accurate, identify authors where appropriate and update content when facts change.

Use internal links and a clear structure

Organise related pages into sensible topic groups. Use descriptive anchor text and link to important pages from relevant content.

Measure what matters

Review impressions, clicks, rankings and conversions. Use the data to find pages that need better intent matching, clearer titles, stronger content or technical fixes.
