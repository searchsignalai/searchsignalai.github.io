Getting Started with Generative Engine Optimization

A practical look at content quality and site basics for the growing range of AI-powered search experiences.

SearchSignal AI7 minute readUpdated October 29, 2026

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is a developing term for work intended to improve how content may be found, understood, and referenced by generative AI search experiences. Tools and systems differ, and no single tactic guarantees that a page will be cited.

Practical starting point: Publish accurate, original, well-organized information on a website that people and automated systems can access.

What is GEO?

GEO generally builds on familiar content and technical practices: answer real questions, explain ideas clearly, support claims, and make pages easy to access. It should be treated as an addition to a sound website strategy, not a replacement for SEO.

1. Publish original, useful content

Offer information that helps readers make a decision or complete a task. Include practical examples, original analysis, first-hand experience when available, and details that add more than a generic summary.

2. Support important claims

Link to reliable primary sources when possible. Name the source of data, provide context, distinguish facts from opinions, and avoid fabricated quotes or statistics. Trust grows when readers can check your work.

3. Make information easy to understand

  • Use clear headings and descriptive page titles.
  • Define specialist terms and acronyms.
  • Use lists for steps and tables for comparisons when they improve understanding.
  • Keep important facts close to the section they explain.
  • Use consistent names and facts across related pages.

4. Keep your site accessible

Ensure important pages load without requiring a login, work on mobile, use valid HTML, and link to one another. Check robots.txt and indexing directives so you do not accidentally block pages you want discovered. Do not publish hidden text intended only for bots.

5. Build a credible site

Provide clear author or publisher information, contact details, editorial standards where useful, and transparent corrections. Earn relevant mentions by publishing material that people have a reason to reference. Avoid buying low-quality links or mass-producing pages with little value.

6. Measure what you can

Track organic search performance and referral traffic, review how people use your pages, and record AI referral visits when your analytics tools expose them. Reporting differs by platform, so there is no universal GEO score that fully captures visibility.

GEO starter checklist

  • Answer a clear audience need.
  • Provide original details and trustworthy sources.
  • Use descriptive headings and plain language.
  • Keep important pages accessible and internally linked.
  • Maintain accurate author and organization information.
  • Review performance and update outdated material.

Frequently asked questions

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. Technical accessibility, helpful content, and clear site structure remain important. GEO focuses on potential visibility in generative search experiences as well.

Can anyone guarantee AI citations?

No. Inclusion depends on systems and queries outside a publisher's control. Focus on accuracy, usefulness, accessibility, and earning trust.
