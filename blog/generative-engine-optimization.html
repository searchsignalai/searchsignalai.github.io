GEO: Optimizing Content for Generative Search

A practical introduction to Generative Engine Optimization, including clear facts, reliable sources, entity context and authorship.

Practical guideSearch visibilityUpdated 2026

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization describes work that makes content easier for generative search systems to retrieve, interpret and potentially reference. It is an emerging practice, and no technique guarantees an AI citation.

Write clear, verifiable information

Use specific language, explain the basis for important claims and link to reliable primary sources where possible. Avoid unsupported statistics and vague superlatives.

Make entities and relationships explicit

Use consistent organisation, product, service and author names. Explain what an entity does and how it relates to other entities.

Demonstrate authorship and expertise

Provide meaningful author information and describe relevant first-hand experience. Add review details when a qualified review genuinely took place.

Structure content for retrieval and reading

Use descriptive titles, clear sections, definitions and concise summaries. Keep related information together and make important pages accessible through internal links.

Build reputation through useful work

Publish original research, useful resources and accurate explanations. Seek relevant editorial mentions and keep public business information consistent.
